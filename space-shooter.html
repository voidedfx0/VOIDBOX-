Ready for launch?

Read the rules before you launch.

• Entry: 0.40 ZEC per run, charged once; continuing has no extra entry fee.
• Normal enemy: 10 points. Level 5 boss: 150 points when defeated (12 hits needed).
• Total score targets: L1 50 · L2 120 · L3 210 · L4 320 · L5 450 + boss.
• You have 45 seconds per level and 3 lives. Miss the target or lose all lives and the run ends.
• Checkpoint payouts are total cash-out amounts: 0.10 · 0.25 · 0.45 · 0.70 · 1.00 ZEC; they do not add together.
• At a checkpoint, Take Win to cash out or Continue to risk the unbanked reward.
• Exit warning: if you have already staked/paid the entry amount, exiting the game forfeits that stake and any unbanked reward.

Prototype only: no real ZEC is currently charged, held, or paid out.
